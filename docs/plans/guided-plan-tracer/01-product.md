# Product: Guided plan tracer

## Problem
People in Trinidad and Tobago cannot quickly compare an individual grocery item's price across nearby trusted stores. They also need a reason to create an account: a persistent grocery list and an understandable plan for shopping it themselves.

## Success metric
In a five-person pilot, at least four people can compare one grocery item's price across nearby stores without assistance, and at least two choose to sign in to create a persistent grocery list and receive its guided plan; measure completed tasks and sign-ins during a moderated session.

## Announcement — the blog post before the feature
You can now search broadly to see matching exact grocery products and their estimated prices, then choose one product to compare it at nearby stores without creating an account. When you are ready to save exact items to a grocery list and receive a clear plan for shopping it yourself, sign in. Every item in the plan shows the selected product, store, and price information used. This first release only uses a deliberately small set of trusted products and stores, so you can understand exactly what each search, comparison, and plan can cover.

## Screens
- `mockups/product-search.html` — broad search results show product photos, matching brands, exact sizes, and estimated prices; choose one exact product before comparison.
- `mockups/item-comparison.html` — compare the selected exact product's eligible store offers with filters and sorting.
- `mockups/sign-in.html` — explain why sign-in is required before creating a persistent grocery list or a guided plan.
- `mockups/grocery-list.html` — signed-in user creates a persistent short grocery list and requests a guided plan.
- `mockups/guided-plan.html` — signed-in user inspects the recommended plan, coverage, item assignments, and data recency.
